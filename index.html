How I work

  • I own it after launch

    Everything I ship, I also watch in production — Datadog and Sentry dashboards, incident triage, root-cause fixes, and the hardening that follows.

  • Slow work goes to the background

    Long-running jobs move onto queues, webhooks, and serverless workers so the product stays responsive when load arrives.

  • AI ships with guardrails

    LLM features go live with prompt orchestration, retries and fallbacks, and explicit budgets for cost and latency.

  • Tested before trusted

    Unit, integration, and end-to-end suites, careful code review, and CI/CD pipelines keep releases repeatable and low-risk.
